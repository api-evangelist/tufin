Tufin Global Field CTO Erez Tadmor sits down with Tufin CISO Jeffrey Spear for a no-fluff hype check on the recent wave of AI security headlines. The conversation argues that the tooling isn't reinventing security but rather collapsing the time and effort of vulnerability research, shrinking the window between vulnerability discovery and exploitation, and positions network controls as the foundational defense layer when other options prove unreliable.
