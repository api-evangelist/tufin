<p>Microsegmentation has long been a cornerstone of zero-trust security, but according to <a href="https://lp.tufin.com/CT_2026_04_21_Gartner_Comp_Intelligence_landing-page.html">new Gartner research</a>, the market is entering a new phase defined by complexity, automation, and rapid innovation.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Three Forces Are Reshaping Microsegmentation</h2>



<p><a href="https://lp.tufin.com/CT_2026_04_21_Gartner_Comp_Intelligence_landing-page.html">Gartner® identifies</a> three primary forces driving change:</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">AI Is Becoming a Key Differentiator</h3>



<p>AI is transforming microsegmentation capabilities, enabling:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Anomaly detection across distributed environments</li>



<li>Dynamic policy adjustments in real time</li>



<li>Deeper insights into application and API behavior</li>
</ul>



<p>But adoption is cautious.</p>



<p>Organizations still require <strong>human oversight, transparency, and strong safeguards</strong> to trust AI-driven decisions.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Cloud and Containers Are Increasing Complexity</h3>



<p>Hybrid and multicloud environments have made segmentation significantly harder.</p>



<p>Challenges include:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Constantly changing workloads</li>



<li>Limited visibility into dependencies</li>



<li>Policy sprawl and misconfigurations</li>
</ul>



<p>This is driving demand for:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Agentless microsegmentation</li>



<li>Real-time topology mapping</li>



<li>Deep cloud-native integrations</li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">CPS/OT Environments Are Expanding the Attack Surface</h3>



<p>As cyber-physical systems grow, so does the need for segmentation. However, most microsegmentation solutions:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Lack native CPS capabilities</li>



<li>Depend on third-party integrations</li>



<li>Struggle with fragmented and proprietary environments</li>
</ul>



<p>This creates a major gap in security strategies.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Trust Gap in Automation</h2>



<p>Perhaps the most striking insight:</p>



<p><strong><a href="https://lp.tufin.com/CT_2026_04_21_Gartner_Comp_Intelligence_landing-page.html">By 2030, 10% of organizations will have sufficient trust to run autonomous agents to segment their networks with no human oversight, up from less than 1% in 2026.</a></strong> This reflects both the promise and the hesitation surrounding AI-driven security.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Organizations Should Do Next</h2>



<p>Gartner recommends focusing on:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>AI-driven risk scoring and compliance</li>



<li>Agentless enforcement across cloud and containers</li>



<li>Unified management with separate policies for IT and CPS</li>



<li>Improved visibility and policy automation</li>
</ul>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Final Takeaway</h2>



<p>Microsegmentation is no longer just about segmentation, it’s about operationalizing zero trust at scale. And the organizations that succeed will be those that can balance:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Automation and control</li>



<li>Visibility and simplicity</li>



<li>Innovation and trust</li>
</ul>
